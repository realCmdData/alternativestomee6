# VoidBox

### 🔍 Overview

VoidBox is an all-in-one bot built to replace the stack most servers end up with: levelling with rank cards, an economy, moderation and anti-raid, tickets, applications, giveaways, reaction roles, welcome and goodbye messages, a starboard, social alerts (YouTube, Reddit, Kick, Bluesky, RSS), stat channels and AI chat, all from one web dashboard.

Its standout feature for anyone leaving MEE6 is level importing: members keep their levels when you switch from MEE6, Lurkr, Amari, Tatsu or Arcane. It also has a free honeypot channel that catches hacked accounts spamming every channel.

The full dashboard is free. There is no dashboard paywall and no trial timer.

### 🤔 Who It's For

Servers that want one bot instead of four, and especially servers moving off MEE6 that don't want their members to lose their levels. The dashboard is made for non-technical admins: most features are switches and dropdowns rather than code.

It is a newer bot than the others on this list, so expect fewer years of track record.

### 💵 Price

Every feature listed above is free, including AI chat (50 replies a day per server on a free model). Paid plans add paid AI models with no daily cap and higher limits, one server at a time.
