# CARTIQO

### 🔍 Overview

CARTIQO is a newer all-in-one bot with a full web dashboard. Protection is on from the moment it joins: anti-nuke stops someone who suddenly deletes channels or roles, mass-bans or hands out admin, and raid lockdown closes the server when too many accounts join at once.

Moderation is where it stands out. Every action is a numbered case, and the bot saves the messages around it *before* they're deleted, with copies of files and a SHA-256 hash, so you can show exactly what happened. On top of that it does verification (button, captcha, quiz or web), tickets (incl. a dropdown menu and ModMail-style DM tickets), levels with rank cards and a MEE6 level import, giveaways, suggestions, polls, applications, role menus, birthdays, social feeds and simple automations ("flows").

It's available in 30 languages with a per-server language setting, is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt), and members can see and delete their own data from the dashboard.

### 🤔 Who's It For

Small and medium servers that want one bot instead of four, and especially servers that care about raids, nukes and being able to back up a moderation decision. Moderation, anti-nuke, raid lockdown, verification, levels with role rewards and the setup wizard are all free.

It's a young bot (in beta in late 2026), so expect active development and the odd rough edge rather than a decade of track record.

### 💵 Price

The core is free. Premium raises the limits (for example 25 ticket panels instead of 1, 25 giveaways at a time instead of 2, 10 social feeds instead of 3) and adds things like custom filter patterns, automatic punishments, XP multipliers and voice XP. Ultimate adds a white-label bot. During the beta every server has Premium for free until 1 January 2027.
